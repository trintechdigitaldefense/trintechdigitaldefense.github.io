A one-time audit is a scoped review of your business security. Depending on the selected tier, it can assess external exposure, ports and services, DNS and email security, SSL/TLS, vulnerabilities, endpoints, firewall and router settings, Wi-Fi, identity and access controls, and available security logs. You receive a written report with prioritised findings and practical recommendations. 24/7 active monitoring is an ongoing monthly service that watches supported systems, uses active deception to surface suspicious activity, and provides alerts and reporting. Monitoring requires a dedicated device on your network and written authorisation.

Mirage places decoys on the network: fake services, honey tokens, and canary files. Sentinel watches the host for file changes, reverse shells, and persistence. If someone opens a decoy, uses a fake credential, or probes a fake service, that raises an alert. Alerts can go to WhatsApp or Telegram, with a written report. Isolate, kill, or block runs through CyberGuardian only after a person approves it. Monitoring needs a signed NDA, written permission, and a device on your network.
