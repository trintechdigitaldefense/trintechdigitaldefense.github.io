01 // What happens next

A clear, controlled intake process

  1. Send a short enquiry.

    Use the homepage enquiry form to prepare a draft email, or contact us through WhatsApp. The website does not receive or store the form fields.

  2. Initial scoping discussion.

    We confirm the service requested, approximate asset count, objectives and practical constraints.

  3. Confidentiality and written scope.

    Agree the NDA, authorised assets, permitted methods, timing and reporting expectations before technical details or testing.

  4. Assessment and reporting.

    Work begins only after the required written permission and scope are in place.

02 // Prepare your request

Useful information for first contact

  • Your name and organisation.
  • The service you are interested in.
  • Approximate number of devices, sites or applications.
  • Your high-level objective and preferred timeframe.
  • Confirmation that you are authorised to request the discussion.
Do not send sensitive technical information in your first enquiry.

Never include passwords, private keys, access tokens, personal records, client data, raw logs, vulnerability evidence or detailed network information through public email or WhatsApp. An enquiry is not permission to test. Use an agreed secure channel after scope and confidentiality arrangements are established.
