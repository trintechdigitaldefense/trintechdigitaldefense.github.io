Terms of Engagement

Last updated: August 2026 · TrinTech Digital Defense, Trinidad & Tobago

1. Scope of Services

All cybersecurity services provided by TrinTech Digital Defense — including vulnerability assessment, OSINT reconnaissance, network audits, and security consulting — are conducted under a formal scope of work agreed upon with the client before any testing begins.

2. Rules of Engagement

All testing is performed within agreed-upon boundaries. The client retains full ownership of their systems. TrinTech does not modify, delete, or alter client data without explicit written permission. All testing is conducted in a controlled manner to minimize operational impact.

3. Confidentiality

A signed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) is required before any engagement begins. All findings, evidence, and client information remain strictly confidential. No findings are shared with third parties without written client permission.

4. Reporting

Each engagement concludes with a written report containing severity ratings, evidence, and remediation recommendations. Reports are delivered only to authorized client personnel as specified in the NDA.

5. Liability

TrinTech Digital Defense exercises professional care in all engagements. While every effort is made to minimize operational impact, clients acknowledge that any security testing carries inherent risk. Liability is limited to the value of the engagement fee.

6. Contact

For questions about these terms, contact us at trintechdigitaldefense@gmail.com.
