Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 · TrinTech Digital Defense, Trinidad & Tobago

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you choose to send us by email or WhatsApp (for example your name, organisation, phone number, and a description of your security needs). The website enquiry form prepares a message in your own email application using a mailto link. The website does not transmit the form fields to a server or store them. You decide whether to send the message; your email provider then processes it. WhatsApp is also an external service; its provider processes messages under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not ask for passwords, private keys, access tokens, personal records, or confidential technical evidence through public channels.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, provide cybersecurity services, and prepare assessments. We do not sell or share your information with third parties without your explicit consent.

3. Non-Disclosure Agreement

Engagements begin with a signed Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA). Findings and technical details from our work are confidential under that NDA.

4. Data Retention

Enquiry data is retained only as long as needed to respond and complete any engagement. You may request deletion by emailing us.

5. Website Enquiries

Only provide basic contact details and a high-level description of the service requested. Do not send credentials, access tokens, personal records, or sensitive system details through the public enquiry form, email, or WhatsApp. Sensitive evidence is exchanged only through a separately agreed process after scope and confidentiality arrangements are established.

6. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: trintechdigitaldefense@gmail.com · WhatsApp +1 (868) 362-0679.
