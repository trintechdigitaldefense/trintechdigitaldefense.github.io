WHAT YOU RECEIVE

  • A review of the business systems and practices we agree in writing.
  • The weaknesses found inside that scope.
  • A plain risk rating.

You do not need to understand cybersecurity to use the report. We say what is wrong, why it matters, and what to do next.

  • Not a free full audit. The Micro-Business Audit (1–5 devices, TT$1,000) and the Small Business Audit (5–15 devices, TT$2,200) stay separate, larger jobs.

  • Not a promise of a certificate, a SOC, or a result we have not delivered.
