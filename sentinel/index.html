FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know before deploying Sentinel.

What does Sentinel monitor?

Everything connected to your network — endpoints (laptops, servers, mobile devices), cloud infrastructure, firewalls, network traffic, and connected APIs. We tailor coverage to your environment during deployment.

How fast is the incident response?

Our target is initial response within 15 minutes of alert. Containment typically follows within 30 minutes. Your exact SLA depends on your plan — Starter, Pro, and Enterprise each include defined response tiers.

Do I need to install software on my devices?

For endpoint coverage, yes — lightweight agents are installed on each monitored device. These consume minimal resources and are designed to be invisible to end users. Network-level monitoring requires no endpoint software.

Can Sentinel integrate with our existing tools?

Yes. Sentinel integrates with SIEM platforms, ticketing systems, Slack/Teams, and custom APIs. Enterprise plans include dedicated integration engineering for complex environments.

Is my data safe with TrinTech?

Absolutely. Every engagement begins with a signed NDA. All monitoring data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We never share your data with third parties. Our team operates under strict confidentiality agreements.

What if we need to scale up or down?

Sentinel plans are flexible. You can upgrade from Starter to Pro, or add endpoints to your existing plan at any time. Downgrades are handled at billing cycle end. No penalties, no lock-in.

Do you serve businesses outside Trinidad & Tobago?

We focus exclusively on Trinidad and Tobago. Remote monitoring works regardless of geography.
