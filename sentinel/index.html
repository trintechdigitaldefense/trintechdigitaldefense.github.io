❓ FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers.

Do I need to be tech-savvy? →

No. TrinTech installs and configures everything for you. You don't need to touch a command line. We handle the technical side — you get the peace of mind.

Does it work for small businesses with just 3-5 computers? →

Absolutely. Sentinel is perfect for small setups. Even with a few computers, you're vulnerable. Hackers target small businesses because they're easier to crack.

Will Sentinel slow down my systems? →

No. Sentinel is designed to be lightweight — it uses zero external libraries and minimal resources. Your network performance won't be affected at all.

What happens when an alert fires? →

You get an immediate alert via email with details: what happened, from where, and what we did (or recommend). Critical alerts include auto-blocked IPs. You can always call us.

Is this legal? →

Sentinel runs exclusively on systems you own or have authorization to monitor. All activities are fully legal under Trinidad & Tobago's Cybercrimes Act and international law.

Can I see the source code? →

Sentinel is open source at github.com/trintechdigitaldefense/Sentinel. But we recommend managed service — configuring and maintaining it yourself takes significant expertise.

What if I get false alerts? →

We fine-tune Sentinel during setup to minimize false positives. If a legitimate device triggers an alert, we add it to your "known devices" list. It learns and improves over time.
